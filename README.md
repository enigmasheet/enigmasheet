## About

I’m a full-stack engineer based in Kathmandu, Nepal, with a focus on **C# and .NET**. At Codebee Nepal, I develop and maintain applications with ASP.NET Core, Entity Framework, SQL Server, and REST APIs. I also build product interfaces with TypeScript, React, Next.js, Astro, and Blazor.

I hold a Bachelor’s degree in Information Technology from Texas College of Management and IT. My earlier experience includes project coordination and robotics education.
